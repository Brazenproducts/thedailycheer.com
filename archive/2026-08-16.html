Teacher's Mentorship Program Graduates 500th Student Into College

In a remarkable milestone, retired teacher Margaret Chen's weekend mentorship program celebrated its 500th college-bound graduate this month. Starting from her kitchen table in 2015, Chen's initiative has grown into a comprehensive program serving low-income students across three counties. This year's cohort includes first-generation college students bound for prestigious universities with full scholarships. 'Every student has potential,' Chen reflects. 'They just need someone to believe in them first.' Her program combines SAT prep, essay coaching, and college navigation support—entirely free. Former graduates now volunteer as mentors, creating a ripple effect of opportunity that extends far beyond the classroom.
