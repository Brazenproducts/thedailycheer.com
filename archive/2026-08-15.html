⭐ Today's Top Story

Teen Volunteers Build 50 Wheelchair Ramps in Summer Drive

Seventeen-year-old Maya Chen organized a grassroots summer initiative called "Ramps for All" that mobilized over 200 high school volunteers across three counties. In just eight weeks, the team designed and built 50 accessible wheelchair ramps for elderly residents and disabled families who couldn't afford professional installation. Working with local hardware stores that donated materials and retired carpenters who mentored the teens, the project cost less than $15,000 while providing an estimated $500,000 in accessibility improvements. Maya credits social media for spreading the word: "I posted about the first ramp on TikTok, and by the next morning, we had 50 people asking how to help." The program has inspired similar initiatives in five neighboring states.
