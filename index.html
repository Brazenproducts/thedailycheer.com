New Gene Therapy Restores Hearing in 47 Children Born Deaf

A groundbreaking clinical trial at Johns Hopkins has successfully restored functional hearing in 47 children with congenital deafness using a single-dose gene therapy. The treatment, which delivers a working copy of the OTOF gene directly to the inner ear, showed results within weeks. Parents reported children responding to their names and music for the first time, with researchers calling it a 'new chapter in audiology.'

Iceland Becomes First Country to Run Entirely on Green Hydrogen

Iceland announced this week that its entire transportation grid, including fishing vessels and domestic flights, now runs exclusively on green hydrogen produced from geothermal energy. The island nation achieved the milestone two years ahead of schedule, eliminating its last fossil fuel dependency.

Wild Tiger Population Crosses 6,000 for First Time in Decades

The Global Tiger Initiative confirmed that wild tiger numbers have reached 6,127, more than doubling since the 2010 low point. India, Nepal, and Bhutan led recovery efforts through habitat corridors and anti-poaching technology that uses drone surveillance.

California Condor Flock Reaches New Milestone with 100 Wild Chicks

Conservationists announced that 100 California condor chicks hatched in the wild this year, a record that was unimaginable when only 22 birds remained in 1982. The species now numbers over 600, with self-sustaining populations in three states.

Luna, a sea otter rescued as an orphaned pup in 2023, gave birth to a healthy female this week at Monterey Bay Aquarium. Staff say Luna is showing excellent maternal instincts, wrapping her pup in kelp while she forages—a behavior she learned from surrogate mothers.
