Eighteen-year-old Maya Chen's solar-powered river filtration system, first prototyped in her garage at age 14, has now been installed across six continents. The flightless bird, once declared extinct in 1898 before a small colony was rediscovered in 1948, now numbers over 500 individuals. Intensive predator control and breeding programs in Fiordland have secured the species' future for the first time in modern history.
