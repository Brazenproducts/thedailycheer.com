Solar-Powered Desalination Plant Brings Water to 500,000 in Kenya

The world's largest solar-powered desalination facility opened today in Turkana County, Kenya, providing clean drinking water to half a million people who previously walked up to six hours daily for water access. The $180 million project, funded by a coalition of international partners and the Kenyan government, uses zero fossil fuels and will expand to serve 1.2 million residents by 2028.

New Alzheimer's Drug Shows 61% Slowdown in Cognitive Decline

Phase 3 trial results published in The Lancet reveal that the medication donanemab-R reduced cognitive decline by 61% in early-stage patients over 18 months. The FDA is expected to fast-track approval, potentially making it available by early 2027.

Paraguay Becomes First South American Nation to Eliminate Malaria

The World Health Organization officially certified Paraguay as malaria-free this week, marking a historic milestone after a 70-year eradication campaign. The country recorded zero local transmission cases for seven consecutive years.

Free Public Transit Pilot in Denver Cuts Car Traffic by 18%

After one year of fare-free buses and light rail, Denver officials report a significant drop in downtown vehicle congestion and a 34% increase in ridership. The city council voted unanimously to extend the program through 2028.

Wild Iberian Lynx Population Surpasses 2,000 for First Time

Spanish and Portuguese conservationists announced the Iberian lynx has officially recovered from the brink of extinction. In 2002, fewer than 100 remained; today, over 2,000 roam the Iberian Peninsula thanks to breeding programs and habitat restoration.

Great Barrier Reef Records High Coral Recovery in Northern Sections

Marine biologists report a 22% increase in coral cover across the northern Great Barrier Reef, the highest recovery rate documented in 15 years. Cooler-than-average ocean temperatures and reduced cyclone activity contributed to the rebound.

Wildlife

Rescued Elephant Calf Released Into Protected Sri Lankan Sanctuary

A three-month-old elephant calf orphaned by poachers was successfully rehabilitated and released into Minneriya National Park this week. Wildlife officials fitted her with a GPS collar to monitor her integration with a wild herd.

Crowdfunded Library Opens in Rural Mississippi Town

Residents of Marks, Mississippi celebrated the grand opening of their first public library, funded entirely by a viral GoFundMe campaign that raised $287,000. The 4,000-square-foot building includes a children's wing and free computer lab.
